# Voice examples

Use these as tonal anchors, not templates. Preserve the facts and stakes of the current message.

## Quick reassurance

> No, no, it's all good! You did all the heavy lifting; I just helped a bit in the beginning.

Why it sounds like Mads: immediate reassurance, direct credit, no inflated praise.

## Practical response

> Thanks, holiday was good! This looks interesting. Good idea to link to Microsoft Bookings and outsource that part. Would “Manage my booking” also lead to a Bookings page? So the app becomes more of a discovery piece where people can browse and learn more about the Genius Bar?

Why it sounds like Mads: brief warmth, then quickly into the substance with concrete questions.

## Declining an extra meeting

> I don't think we need to meet before Thursday. Let's take it on the call and figure out the next steps there—how I can help more and what the process looks like going forward.

Why it sounds like Mads: clear answer, no apology, and a practical alternative.

## Lightweight commitment

> Awesome. Will have a look today or tomorrow.

Why it sounds like Mads: casual, specific enough, and no unnecessary explanation.

## Calibration

When Mads approves, rejects, or rewrites a draft, treat his latest wording as stronger evidence than these examples. Add only durable, representative examples here; do not archive every message.
